# Linear systems in tensor formats

The solution of linear systems $A u = b$ whose operator, right-hand side and
solution are in a low-rank format: iterative methods with truncation, greedy
and alternating minimization algorithms, the solvers in tensor-train format,
and the approximation of the inverse for preconditioning.
